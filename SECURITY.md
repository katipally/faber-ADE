# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability

Email yashwanthreddykatipally@gmail.com with "Faber security" in the subject. Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

Include:

- The Faber version and macOS version.
- What you found and the steps to reproduce it.
- What an attacker could do with it.

Do not send real secrets, private code or other people's data. If you need an encrypted channel, ask in your first email.

I will reply as soon as I can. I will tell you when a fix ships and, if you want, credit you in the release notes.

## Scope

In scope:

- The Faber macOS app and the Faber Companion iPhone app.
- The update feed and release assets in this repository.
- Community mods in `mods/` and the mod sandbox and consent flow.

Out of scope:

- The coding agents Faber launches (Claude Code, Codex and others) and their providers.
- Third-party mods outside this repository.
- Issues that need a device you do not control, or physical access to an unlocked Mac.
- Denial of service through very large local inputs.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release gets security fixes. Faber updates itself, so please update before reporting.

## Safe harbor

Good-faith research that follows this policy, avoids privacy harm and service disruption, and gives us time to fix the issue before disclosure will not be pursued.
